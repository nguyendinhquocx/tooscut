Tooscut is a browser-native, non-linear video editor powered by WebGPU and Rust/WASM. It delivers desktop-class performance with nothing to install.

## Why Tooscut

- **GPU-accelerated compositing** — All rendering happens in a Rust/wgpu pipeline compiled to WASM.
- **Runs entirely in the browser** — No uploads, no servers. Your media stays on your machine.
- **Built for speed** — Instant playback, scrubbing, and export.
